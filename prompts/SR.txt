* Use the following guidelines to pick exactly one label:
    Simple:
        Involves only one table.
        No JOINs or subqueries.
        At most one basic filter (e.g., WHERE x > 0) or aggregation (COUNT, SUM, etc.).
        No GROUP BY, no window functions.
        Example: “How many rows are in table demo.equipment?”

    Difficult:
        Involves 2–3 tables with up to 2 JOINs.
        May include a GROUP BY, a single subquery, or a HAVING clause.

        Involves 4+ tables or nested JOINs (JOINs inside subqueries).

        Includes advanced computation, ranking, comparisons over time, or conditional aggregation.

        Example: “Find the top 5 customers in each WERKS by total volume, excluding days with no shipments, and compute a 7-day moving average.”
